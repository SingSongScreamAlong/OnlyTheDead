## ORDER OF THE DAY
### April 10, 1916

**To all ranks of the Second Army:**

The 9th of April attack, brilliantly executed by the XX Corps and the II Colonial Corps, has inflicted a heavy defeat upon the enemy.

I wish to express to the generals, officers, non-commissioned officers, and soldiers who participated in it the pride that I feel in commanding such troops.

You have not only stopped the most formidable effort that the Germans have yet attempted, but you have broken it and thrown them back.

**The Germans will attack again.**

Let every man be determined to give as good an account of himself as his comrades have just done.

**Courage!**

**On les aura!** (We'll get them!)

---

**Signed**,
Philippe Pétain
General of Division
Commander, Second Army

---

**Historical Context**:

This order was issued following a successful French counterattack on April 9, 1916, which temporarily halted German advances near Mort-Homme. It exemplifies Pétain's command style: direct praise for soldiers, realistic acknowledgment of future challenges, and the now-famous rallying cry "On les aura!" (We'll get them!).

The phrase "On les aura" became synonymous with French determination at Verdun and was widely reproduced on propaganda posters throughout 1916-1918.
