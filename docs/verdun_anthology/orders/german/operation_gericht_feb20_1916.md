# OPERATION ORDER: GERICHT
## German Fifth Army

**From**: Headquarters, German Fifth Army
**To**: All Corps Commanders, Verdun Offensive Sector
**Date**: February 20, 1916
**Time**: 18:00 hours
**Classification**: GEHEIM (SECRET) - Declassified 1956
**Operation Code Name**: GERICHT (Judgment)

---

## OBERKOMMANDO DER 5. ARMEE
### Stenay, 20. Februar 1916

### OPERATIONSBEFEHL NR. 1
### (OPERATION ORDER NO. 1)

---

### I. ABSICHT (INTENTION)

Fifth Army will attack French positions on the right bank of the Meuse River in the Verdun sector with the objective of fixing French forces and inflicting maximum casualties.

The operation is predicated on the principle of **Ausblutung** (bleeding white): the French will be compelled to defend Verdun for reasons of national prestige and strategic necessity. Superior German artillery will destroy French forces faster than they can be replaced.

**End State**: French Army combat effectiveness reduced to the point of collapse or negotiated peace.

---

### II. LAGE (SITUATION)

#### A. Enemy Forces

French forces in Verdun sector estimated at 4 divisions (approximately 60,000 men). Fortifications partially disarmed (heavy guns removed in 1915). Morale reported as adequate but not high. French command has neglected Verdun sector in favor of offensive preparations elsewhere.

**Assessment**: French are vulnerable.

#### B. Friendly Forces

Fifth Army has concentrated:
- **Infantry**: 6.5 divisions (72 battalions)
- **Artillery**: 1,220 guns (including 13x 420mm, 17x 305mm Škoda)
- **Ammunition**: 2,500,000 shells pre-positioned
- **Aviation**: 168 aircraft (superiority established)
- **Engineers**: 3 pioneer regiments (assault, flamethrowers, demolitions)

**Assessment**: We have overwhelming superiority in artillery and local infantry strength.

---

### III. AUFTRAG (MISSION)

#### Phase I: Preparation Fire (H-9 hours to H-hour)
**Date**: 21 February 1916
**H-Hour**: 16:00 hours
**Duration**: 9 hours (07:15-16:00)

All artillery will engage in systematic destruction of French positions on a frontage of 20 kilometers:

- **Targets**:
  - Front-line trenches (Bois des Caures, Herbebois, Haumont)
  - Communication trenches
  - Command posts
  - Artillery positions
  - Approach roads
  - Village strongpoints (Haumont, Samogneux, Beaumont)

- **Method**: Progressive bombardment, moving forward in timed increments
- **Munitions**: Mix of high explosive and shrapnel
- **Objective**: Complete destruction of French defensive capacity

#### Phase II: Infantry Assault (H-hour)
**Time**: 16:00 hours, 21 February

Infantry assault will commence on a narrow front (Bois des Caures to Ornes):

- **Right Wing**: VII Reserve Corps (Gen. von Zwehl)
- **Center**: XVIII Corps (Gen. von Schenck)
- **Left Wing**: III Corps (Gen. von Lochow)

**Method**:
- Storm troops advance with flamethrowers and grenades
- Follow-on waves consolidate captured positions
- No pause—maintain momentum

**Objective Day 1**: Capture Bois des Caures, Herbebois, Haumont
**Objective Day 2**: Reach line Samogneux-Beaumont
**Objective Day 3**: Capture Fort Douaumont

#### Phase III: Exploitation (D+3 onward)

Upon capture of Douaumont, forces will consolidate and prepare for French counterattacks. Artillery will shift to interdiction and counter-battery missions.

**Subsequent objectives** (timing dependent on French response):
- Fort Vaux
- Souville Ridge
- Verdun Citadel (if French collapse permits)

---

### IV. AUSFÜHRUNG (EXECUTION)

#### A. Artillery Fire Plan (Detailed)

**07:15-09:00**: Saturation fire on front-line trenches
**09:00-11:00**: Interdiction fire on communication trenches and supply routes
**11:00-13:00**: Counter-battery fire (French guns located via aerial reconnaissance)
**13:00-15:00**: Final preparation fire, forward trenches
**15:00-16:00**: Rolling barrage, advancing 100m per 5 minutes

**Ammunition Allocation**:
- Heavy howitzers (210mm, 150mm): Fort positions, dugouts
- Field guns (77mm): Trenches, wire, troops
- Gas shells: Selected targets (woods, ravines where gas will concentrate)

**Fire Control**: All batteries will fire on command from army artillery headquarters. No independent fire without authorization.

#### B. Infantry Tactics

**Storm Troops** (Sturmtruppen):
- Lead assault with grenades, flamethrowers, and close-combat weapons
- Objective: Overwhelm survivors of bombardment
- Bypass strongpoints for follow-on waves

**Follow-On Waves**:
- Consolidate captured positions
- Mop up bypassed positions
- Establish defensive perimeter against counterattacks

**Reserves**:
- V Reserve Corps held in second line
- Commit only on army commander's order

#### C. Engineer Support

Pioneer battalions will:
- Clear obstacles and wire
- Deploy flamethrowers against bunkers
- Construct approach trenches under fire
- Prepare demolitions for Fort Douaumont assault

#### D. Aviation

Luftstreitkräfte will:
- Maintain air superiority
- Conduct artillery observation
- Strafe French reinforcements on approach roads
- Photograph results of bombardment

---

### V. VERWALTUNG (ADMINISTRATION & LOGISTICS)

#### A. Supply

Ammunition trains will maintain continuous resupply via Metz-Stenay railway. Priority: artillery shells, grenades, small arms ammunition.

Medical services: Field hospitals established at Spincourt, Damvillers, Stenay. Expect heavy casualties first 48 hours.

#### B. Replacements

Infantry replacement battalions held at Montmédy. Commit as needed to maintain offensive strength.

---

### VI. FÜHRUNG UND NACHRICHTENVERBINDUNG (COMMAND & COMMUNICATIONS)

#### A. Command Posts

- **Fifth Army HQ**: Stenay (forward CP: Azannes)
- **Corps HQs**: As per separate orders
- **Forward Observers**: Attached to assault battalions

#### B. Communications

- Telegraph: Primary (lines buried where possible)
- Telephone: Secondary (expect lines cut by French artillery)
- Visual signals: Flares for phase line objectives (green = objective reached)
- Runners: Backup method

#### C. Reports

All corps commanders will report to Fifth Army HQ:
- Every 2 hours during bombardment
- Every hour during assault
- Immediately upon reaching phase line objectives
- Immediately upon encountering significant resistance

---

### VII. BESONDERE ANWEISUNGEN (SPECIAL INSTRUCTIONS)

#### A. Prisoners

French prisoners will be disarmed and sent to rear immediately. Intelligence officers will interrogate for information on French reserves and morale.

#### B. Casualties

German casualties will be evacuated via designated routes. Medical personnel will not advance with assault waves (exception: storm troop medics).

#### C. Gas Warfare

Gas shells authorized for use against woods and ravines. **Warning**: Wind must be favorable (west to east). Meteorological officer will advise. Protective masks mandatory for all troops.

#### D. Discipline

Looting is forbidden. Summary execution authorized for infractions that compromise operational security or discipline.

---

### VIII. NACHRICHTENSPERRE (INFORMATION SECURITY)

This operation order is classified GEHEIM (SECRET). Compromise will result in court-martial.

Lower-level orders will contain only information necessary for specific units. Soldiers will not be informed of operation objectives beyond their immediate mission.

Press censorship is in effect. No mention of Verdun offensive until officially announced by OHL (Oberste Heeresleitung / Supreme Army Command).

---

### IX. SCHLUSSGEDANKE (CLOSING THOUGHT)

Soldiers of the Fifth Army,

Tomorrow we begin an operation that will determine the outcome of the war. The French believe Verdun is impregnable. They believe their fortifications and their courage will stop us.

They are wrong.

We possess superior firepower, superior training, and superior will. We will demonstrate to France—and to the world—that German arms are invincible.

The Fatherland expects every man to do his duty. Forward, for Kaiser and Reich!

---

**Signed**,

**Wilhelm**
Kronprinz des Deutschen Reiches und von Preußen
General der Infanterie
Kommandierender General, 5. Armee

**Countersigned**,

**Schmidt von Knobelsdorf**
Generalleutnant
Chef des Generalstabes, 5. Armee

---

**Historical Note**: This order synthesizes the actual German operational plan for the Verdun offensive. While not a verbatim document, it accurately reflects:
- The Ausblutung (attrition) strategy attributed to Falkenhayn
- The artillery fire plan (1,220 guns, 9-hour bombardment)
- The narrow-front assault doctrine
- Tactical methods (storm troops, flamethrowers, gas)
- Command structure (Crown Prince Wilhelm as nominal commander, Knobelsdorf as operational brain)

Key details are documented in German Reichsarchiv records and post-war memoirs by German commanders. The operation began as planned on February 21, 1916 (delayed from February 12 due to weather). Original German operational orders remain partially classified; this document is a historical reconstruction based on declassified sources. Primary sources held at Bundesarchiv-Militärarchiv, Freiburg.
