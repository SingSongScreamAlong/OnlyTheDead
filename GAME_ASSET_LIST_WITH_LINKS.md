# ⚠️ IMPORTANT NOTES

## Licensing
- **Editorial Use:** Some historical figures may require releases
- **Commercial Use:** Ensure all purchased assets have commercial license
- **Resale:** Most marketplaces prohibit reselling assets as-is
- **Attribution:** Check if free assets require credit

## Technical Requirements
- **UE5 Compatibility:** Verify all UE assets work with your engine version
- **Polygon Counts:** Consider performance (target 50-100k tris per character)
- **Texture Resolution:** 4K for hero assets, 2K for background, 1K for distant
- **File Formats:** Prefer FBX for models, WAV for audio

## Quality Control
- **Test Before Buying:** Use preview images/videos carefully
- **Read Reviews:** Check buyer feedback on CGTrader/TurboSquid
- **Request Refunds:** Most marketplaces allow refunds for broken assets
- **Version Compatibility:** Ensure assets work with UE5, not just UE4
