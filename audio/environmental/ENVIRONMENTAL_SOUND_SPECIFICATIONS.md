# ENVIRONMENTAL SOUND SPECIFICATIONS

**Project**: Only The Dead - Battle of Verdun 1916
**Category**: Environmental & Atmospheric Audio
**Priority**: HIGH - Environmental sounds create immersion and oppression
**Context**: Verdun's environment was as deadly as the enemy

---

## 🌍 ENVIRONMENTAL PHILOSOPHY

**Verdun's environment was a weapon**:
- Mud that could drown soldiers
- Rain that never stopped
- Cold that killed as surely as bullets
- Rats, lice, and flies as constant companions
- Destroyed landscape = alien, hostile world

**Audio Goal**: Make the environment feel oppressive, exhausting, and inescapable

---

## 🌧️ WEATHER SOUNDS (CRITICAL)

### RAIN (Most Important Weather)
**Historical Context**: Verdun 1916 had exceptional rainfall. Trenches were often flooded.

#### Light Rain
**Characteristics**:
- Gentle patter on helmet (metal ping)
- Soft patter on canvas (tarps, tents)
- Dripping from trench walls
- Puddle forming sounds

**Variations Needed**: 6-8 loops (2-3 minutes each)
