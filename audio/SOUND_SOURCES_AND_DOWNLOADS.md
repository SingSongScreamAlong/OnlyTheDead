## 🎤 CUSTOM RECORDING OPPORTUNITIES

### When to Record Custom Sounds:

1. **Mud sounds** - Specific to Verdun environment
   - Record at muddy location
   - Walking, squelching, boots extracting
   - Various depths

2. **Foley** - Equipment sounds
   - Military surplus gear (web belts, canteens)
   - Metal equipment rattling
   - Canvas/fabric sounds

3. **Environmental** - Local recording
   - Rain on various surfaces
   - Wind through structures
   - Water dripping

### Recording Tips:
- Use quality field recorder (Zoom H5/H6, Tascam DR-40X)
- Record at 48kHz, 24-bit WAV
- Get multiple takes (minimum 5 per sound)
- Record "room tone" for each location
- Vary intensity and timing
- Use windscreen for outdoor recording

## 🔊 SOUND SYNTHESIS (Last Resort)

### Sounds That Can Be Synthesized:

1. **Artillery whistles** - Using audio plugins
   - Start with oscillator
   - Apply doppler shift
   - Add wind noise layer
   - Filter for distance

2. **Explosions** - Layer existing sounds
   - Bass thump (sub-100Hz)
   - Mid crack (1-5kHz)
   - High detail (5-10kHz)
   - Add reverb/echo

3. **Ambient loops** - Combining elements
   - Layer multiple sounds
   - Create evolving textures
   - Add environmental reverb

### Recommended Plugins:
- **Whoosh** (Native Instruments) - For projectile sounds
- **R-BASS** (Waves) - For explosion sub-bass
- **Doppler Dome** (Krotos) - For Doppler effects
- **Weaponiser** (Krotos) - For weapon sound design

## 🎧 QUALITY CONTROL

### Evaluate Each Sound:

✅ **Checklist**:
- [ ] Format: WAV or high-bitrate MP3 (320kbps minimum)
- [ ] Sample rate: 44.1kHz or higher (48kHz ideal)
- [ ] Bit depth: 16-bit minimum (24-bit ideal)
- [ ] Duration: Sufficient length (not cut off)
- [ ] Quality: No distortion, clipping, or artifacts
- [ ] Background: Minimal background noise
- [ ] Period-appropriate: No modern sounds (helicopters, jets, etc.)

### Red Flags (Reject These):
❌ Modern weapon sounds (assault rifles, etc.)
❌ Digital/synthetic sounds (unless deliberately synthesized)
❌ Obvious movie/game sounds (overused stock sounds)
❌ Anachronistic sounds (wrong era)
❌ Low quality (hiss, distortion, compression artifacts)
