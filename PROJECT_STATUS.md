## 🎯 COMPETITIVE ADVANTAGES

**What Makes This Game Unique**:

1. **Historical Depth**: 303 days, 35 missions, real locations, 135,000 words of research
2. **Survival Over Combat**: Not an FPS - survival horror in historical setting
3. **Educational Value**: Can be used in schools, museums, military academies
4. **Psychological Depth**: Shell shock, trauma, humanity in war
5. **Respect for Subject**: 700,000 casualties honored, not exploited
6. **Implementation-Ready**: Complete specs, ready for production

**Similar Games (Market Analysis)**:
- This War of Mine (civilian survival) - ✅ Success
- Verdun/Tannenberg (WWI multiplayer) - ✅ Moderate success
- Valiant Hearts (WWI narrative) - ✅ Success
- **Gap**: No single-player WWI survival game with this depth

**Market**: Historical game enthusiasts + survival game fans + educators

## 🎖️ DEDICATION

This project honors the approximately 700,000 French and German soldiers who became casualties at Verdun between February 21 and December 18, 1916.

*"Ils ne passeront pas." (They shall not pass)*

May we remember their sacrifice, understand their suffering, and never forget the cost of war.
